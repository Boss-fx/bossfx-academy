## 1. TOPICS — the curriculum (beginner-safe, Nigerian context)

### DAY 1 — How the forex market really works (Fri, 7:00 PM · ~75 min)
Goal: demystify the market and kill the get-rich-quick myth.
1. What is forex? Money changing hands — the "Mallam / BDC on a global scale" analogy.
2. Currency pairs explained: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, and why price moves (supply, demand, news).
3. How a trade makes or loses money: pips, lots, buy vs sell, in plain terms.
4. Who's on the other side: banks, institutions, retail — why "the market" is not your friend or enemy.
5. The hard truth: the honest success rate, why 90% lose, and what the 10% do differently (process, not prediction).
6. Scam-proofing: signal-seller red flags, "account manager" fraud, fake FX EAs, guaranteed-return lies. (Ties to your blog: *Spot a Scam Forex EA*.)
7. Tools of a real trader: a broker (regulated), a chart (TradingView), a journal, a plan.

### DAY 2 — Risk management & trading psychology (Sat, 4:00 PM · ~75 min)
Goal: the session that actually keeps them in the game.
1. The 1% rule: never risk more than 1–2% of your account on a single trade — with naira examples.
2. Stop-loss & take-profit: deciding your exit *before* you enter.
3. Risk-to-reward: why 1:2 means you can be right less than half the time and still grow.
4. Position sizing: a simple formula anyone can do on a phone calculator.
5. Psychology: fear, greed, revenge trading, FOMO — the danfo/stop-hunt and "ajo discipline" analogies.
6. The trading journal: why writing trades down beats any indicator (tie to your `resources/journals/trading-journal.html`).
7. Building a routine that survives real life (school, 9–5, data costs).

### DAY 3 — Live chart walkthrough + your next steps (Sun, 4:00 PM · ~75 min)
Goal: make it real, then hand them the path forward.
1. Live on TradingView: setting up a clean chart (candlesticks, one timeframe, no clutter).
2. Reading a candlestick: what one candle tells you.
3. Support & resistance: the only two lines a beginner needs first.
4. A simple, rule-based setup walked through end-to-end (entry, stop, target) — *as an example, not a signal*.
5. Demo-first: why they place zero real-money trades for the first weeks.
6. A 30-day beginner plan (what to study, practise, journal each week).
7. **Your next step with BossFx:** Forex 101 (Module 1 free → full course), the Telegram community, and how mentorship works. Soft CTA + Q&A.

## 2. STRUCTURE — run-of-show (repeat the shape each day)

Each 75-min session, minute-by-minute:

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | **Pre-roll** | Holding slide + music, "we start at X, drop your city in the chat." Reduces no-shows leaving early. |
| 0:05–0:10 | **Welcome + housekeeping** | Who you are, Oshodi Youth partnership, compliance line, how Q&A works, "today you'll be able to ___." |
| 0:10–0:15 | **Recap + hook** | (Day 2/3) 60-sec recap of yesterday, then today's one big promise. |
| 0:15–0:55 | **Teaching (3 mini-blocks)** | ~12 min each, one idea per block, each ending in a 1-question poll or "type YES if that makes sense." |
| 0:55–1:05 | **Live demo / worked example** | Share screen: TradingView (Day 3), or a position-size calc (Day 2). |
| 1:05–1:15 | **Q&A + today's action step** | Answer 3–5 questions, give one homework task, tease tomorrow. |
| 1:15 | **Close** | Compliance line, "see you tomorrow 4PM," drop the warm-up link (Forex 101 Module 1 free). |

Facilitation rules that keep beginners engaged online:
- **One idea per slide.** Big text, dark background (your brand is already set up for this).
- **Interaction every ~10 minutes** (poll, "type 1 or 2", name a city). Keeps WhatsApp/Telegram data-light viewers awake.
- **Analogies over jargon.** Always define a term the first time (pip, lot, stop).
- **No live real-money trades, ever.** Everything is "an example for learning."
- **Record every session** and send the replay on WhatsApp/Telegram within the hour — most registrants will watch late.
